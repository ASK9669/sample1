ASTU Leadership Signs Key Performance Indicators

Adama Science and Technology University (ASTU) solidified its commitment to institutional excellence and accountability through performing key activities on October 26, 2025, as President Dr. Lemi Guta and the university vice presidents and their respective officials signed Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) contract at the Pyramid Hotels and Resorts in Bishoftu.

The strategic signing event marks the formal agreement by the university leadership to meet expected success and efficiency in the current academic year. In his welcoming remarks, Dr. Lemi urged "all the managing council members to efficiently perform as per the agreement, stressing that the KPIs are essential tools for achieving ASTU's long-term strategic vision.

KPIs Cascade Through University Structure

The commitment to accountability was systematically cascaded through the university structure. Following the President's signing, the three Vice Presidents and CEO of the president office have formalized agreements with their respective office heads.

Dr. Teshome Abdo, Vice President for Academic Affairs, signed KPIs with his College Deans, Directors and Executives. Dr. Lema Beresa, Vice President for Research and Technology Transfer, formalized agreements with his Deans, Directors and executives. Dr. Solomon Tiruneh, Vice President for Administration and Development, signed the KPIs with his Dean, and Executives. Professor Yadesa Melaku, chief executive officer of the president office has signed the agreement with executive officers.
